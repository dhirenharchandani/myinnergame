At a glance

One gathering, scoped as a single stage.

Day 1 is leadership alignment: values, vision, and the shift from department-first to company-first thinking. Day 2 is team bonding for the full group. Work outside this scope, including the March and May dates raised in conversation, is priced separately once those objectives are defined.

  • 01Replace department-first and commission-first thinking with company-first ownership, named directly against the live stock and cash-flow example.
  • 02Enrol the leadership team in the growth direction so every person understands how their role drives it, without requiring disclosure of confidential figures.
  • 03Build enough cross-department trust that the obstacles raised in the room get solved collaboratively instead of escalated around.
01 · Day One

Leadership alignment

Run as two linked sessions on the same day: a ~25–30 person session for managers and department leads, and a tighter 5–6 person session for the core leadership group.

  • 1.1Values-in-action: company values presented through real internal stories with named contributors, not a slide of words.Both
  • 1.2Growth plan enrolment: vision and direction shared so every leader sees how their role connects to it. No confidential figures required.Both
  • 1.3Collaboration module: why the growth target is unreachable without cross-department trust.Both
  • 1.4Company-first reframe: moves the room from department/commission-first thinking to shared ownership, built around the current stock and cash-flow tension as the live case.Both
  • 1.5Rapid-fire check-in: every leader heard once; shared obstacles surfaced and named back to the room.Both
  • 1.6Leadership commitments: each person in the core group leaves with one written, named commitment.Leadership only
Accepted when
  • Every attendee has spoken at least once in the room.
  • The most common obstacles named in the check-in are documented and shared back to the group.
  • Each member of the core leadership group leaves with one written commitment.
02 · Day Two

Team bonding

Full group, all ~55–60 attendees together. Built around the trust exercise Dhiren ran at his own recent retreat, briefed, run, and debriefed for a group this size.

  • 2.1Facilitated trust exercise, briefed and run for the full group.Full group
  • 2.2Group debrief connecting the exercise explicitly back to the company-first theme from Day 1.Full group
Accepted when
  • Every attendee completes the exercise.
  • The debrief explicitly ties the exercise back to the Day 1 themes.
