Building the AI AR Coordinator

What the firm needs in hand to automate billing, statements of account and collections, built from the 2026 Billing & Collection process and the team worksheet of 18/10/2026.

How it fits together

Systems and the review gate

The coordinator reads from the firm's systems and produces drafts. Nothing reaches a client until the FM signs it off. Lawyers, FEs and the HOD keep their approvals.

Scope

Three phases, lowest risk first

PHASE 1 · START HERE

Statements and collections

  • Monthly SOAs from Zoho
  • Custom SOAs by matter, department or client
  • Weekly aging report
  • Payment reminder drafts

Fixes the starred pain point, SOA delays. It reads from Zoho and sends nothing on its own, so it's easy to verify.

PHASE 2

Invoice preparation

  • Fixed-fee and stage invoices from LOEs
  • Month-end stage check-ins with lawyers
  • Translation, courier and publication matching
  • Disbursement reconciliation

Needs the LOE register built and lawyers in the loop to confirm stages.

PHASE 3

Client questions and reporting

  • Answers to invoice questions
  • Time-log analysis
  • FE reports within AR
  • Mid-engagement changes

Needs the most judgment, so it comes last.

Hourly invoices keep their approval chain (FE → HOD). The coordinator prepares the drafts and chases the approvals. It never approves.
